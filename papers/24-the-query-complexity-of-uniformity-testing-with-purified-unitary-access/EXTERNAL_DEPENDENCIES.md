# External dependencies and scope

Mathlib is pinned to `f897ebcf72cd16f89ab4577d0c826cd14afaafc7`; Lean is 4.24.0. The global axiom list is only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. Detailed interfaces/references are in `external-dependencies.json`. Four published inputs are explicit propositions with no supplied inhabitants:

1. `PUT.External.DirichletJointMomentLaw` (`PUT/ExternalMoments.lean`): for d≥2, a>0, every actual normalized-independent-Gamma monomial is integrable and its Bochner integral is the rising-factorial quotient. The specialization follows from DasGupta, Graduate Probability §1.23 Theorem 1.68 and the NIST DLMF 5.14.2 beta-simplex integral; it is an inference from those formulas. Centered moments, TV, posterior estimates, adversary and query bounds are internal.
2. `PUT.External.NinefoldAmplificationLaw` (`PUT/ExternalAlgorithms.lean`): nine independent coherent repetitions and reversible majority produce an actual tester at 9 Q calls, reducing each promised error to at most 2^9·(.01)^5. This specializes Watrous, Quantum Computational Complexity §§III.3/IV.2; exact 9 Q is a stated specialization, not a literal quotation of Proposition 3. The elementary error arithmetic is internal.
3. `PUT.External.KutinCollisionLaw` (`PUT/CollisionTester.lean`): a positive constant lower bound kappa·(D/4)^(1/3) for actual standard additive-function-query algorithms distinguishing injections from four-to-one functions for positive D divisible by 4, error≤1/3. Source: Kutin, Theory of Computing 1(2005), Theorem 1.1 and §2.1. The exact purification, full controlled/inverse replacement, 4 Q simulation, TV identity and small-d rounding are internal.
4. `PUT.External.AdaptiveNormalizationLaw` (`PUT/AdaptiveModel.lean`): every finite adaptive family with syntactic branch depth≤Q has a fixed unitary tester at 2 Q calls preserving rejection probability on every full valid oracle, including off-promise inputs. This is deferred measurement/history purification with forward/inverse slots, inferred from Watrous §III.3/Figure 5. Promise-only truncation and absorption of factor 2 are proved internally.

Two approved secondary exemptions remain unproved: the previously published Canonne–Kothari–O’Donnell purified-access tester upper bound (TQC 2025 Theorem 1/AppendixA) and the resulting tight complexity, and replacement of.99 success by any fixed success>1/2 using generic amplification. The specific ninefold input above remains independently disclosed; these exemptions do not waive any own lower-bound step.

`PUT.theorem1` is a retained legacy positivity-of-target theorem, not the query lower bound. The actual operational endpoints are in `PUT.Paper` and the 12-target Comparator contract. Whole radial sectors, actual operator norms, genuine prior measures and finite circuit/register embeddings are retained.

The shared release tooling installs the pinned official Comparator. Comparator challenge modules may contain specification placeholders; these are isolated from production proofs. Negative controls intentionally contain an added premise or an illegal axiom and are verification fixtures. No new axioms or proof placeholders were introduced by release curation.
